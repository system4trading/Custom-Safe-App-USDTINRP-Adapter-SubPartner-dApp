Trade INRP / USDT

Direct trades require a valid registered partner referral code. The code resolves to a partner wallet; the adapter receives that wallet for referral attribution.
Buy / Sell submits only the trade and requires an existing allowance. Approve exact amount submits approval only. For an EOA, Approve + Buy / Sell waits for approval confirmation before sending the trade. In Safe, it submits approval and trade together as one Safe proposal. Unlimited approval is optional.

Advanced partner integration

A customer authorization is an EIP-712 signature over the configured adapter address and trade parameters. The partner submits the signed authorization through the routed trade function. The customer must separately approve the required token allowance.

EOA customer signing uses the connected wallet. Safe customer signing depends on the Safe wallet's support for the requested typed-data signing method and must be verified in the target Safe environment.
