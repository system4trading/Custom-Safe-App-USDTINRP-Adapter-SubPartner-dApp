Safe signing note
Safe Apps transaction proposals are supported for contract transactions below. EIP-712 signing from a Safe is not enabled in this build until the specific Safe signing method and EIP-1271 behavior are tested with your Safe deployment. Use an EOA customer wallet for creating signed authorizations for now. Safe can still be the partner submitter if the Safe Apps transaction proposal flow is available.
