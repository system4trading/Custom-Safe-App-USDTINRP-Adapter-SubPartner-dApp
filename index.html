Advanced Partner Integration

A registered partner can submit a trade for a customer using the customer's EIP-712 authorization. The customer must separately approve the input token allowance.

EOA customers sign with EIP-712. Safe customers require a Safe-compatible typed-data signature and on-chain EIP-1271 validation. Safe signing is attempted through the Safe provider; test it with your deployed Safe before production use.
